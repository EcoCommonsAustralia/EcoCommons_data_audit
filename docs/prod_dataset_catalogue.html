The EcoCommons Production Data Catalogue

A browsable inventory of production data holdings, by category and collection

A browsable inventory of everything in the EcoCommons production environment — 58,424 dataset records across 54 collections — profiled by category, time domain, resolution and more.
Author

Xiang Zhao

Published

October 6, 2026

Contact details: support@ecocommons.org.au

Purpose

A single, plain-language answer to the question “what data do we actually have?” — the complete inventory pulled from the EcoCommons production environment, summarised by category and collection, with a compact per-collection profile available to download.

This page is the companion data to the production catalogue audit, which explains how the list was extracted (via POST /api/es/search/dataset, paginated on uuid). Here we just show what came back.

Important

Records are not products. The headline count of 58,424 is a layer-level number — one variable, for one model, one scenario, one month is a single record. A single product, WorldClim v2.1 future climate, expands into 52,308 records (≈90% of the catalogue). For a “what do we have” view, the collection-level breakdown below is the meaningful one; the raw record count is dominated by that combinatorial expansion.

Download the summaries

The meaningful “what do we have” information is published here as two small, text-based files. The full 58,424-row record list and the grouped Excel workbook are not hosted on this site — they are large binaries and every number worth reporting is already rolled up below. They can be regenerated on demand with extract_prod_datasets.py (see the audit).

The long tail matters for governance: 289 records sit in no collection (the None orphan bucket), and many products exist as a single layer. These are exactly the datasets DS-002 is designed to catch. The full download set (downloadable: No) is concentrated in WorldClim-v2.1 and eBird — the two biggest products — which DS-004 flags for an explicit access decision.

How this connects to the Rule Book

This catalogue is the evidence base for the Rule Book: the category vocabulary, the time-domain values, the collection-membership checks, and the “Current status” figures in each rule all come from the numbers on this page. Re-pulling the catalogue and re-checking it against the rules is how we measure whether the platform’s metadata quality is improving over time.
